A Big City

Water Damage Across A Large, Mixed-Age City

Aurora had 386,261 residents in 2020. The city lies in Arapahoe County with 336,035 residents, Adams County with 47,720 and Douglas County with 2,506. A city this size is not one kind of housing, so a leak in one neighborhood may look nothing like a leak in another.

Neighborhoods built in different eras have different plumbing. A newer subdivision may have plastic supply lines and tight construction that traps moisture, while an older house may have original supply lines, galvanized pipe or an aging water heater. Check a building's age and ask what the lines are made of before assuming where a leak started.

Age also changes the repair. Older walls may hold plaster, thicker framing and materials that need extra care, and newer ones may have engineered flooring that swells when wet. The crew reads the building first and then plans the drying.

Storms And Basements

Storms, Creeks And Basements

Sand Creek crosses the Aurora city limit in the northwest corner. Homes near a creek can see higher groundwater and surface water after heavy rain, so a damp basement there may be groundwater entry rather than a plumbing leak.

Window wells and grading cause many basement calls. A window well that fills with rain can leak through the glass or frame, and soil that slopes toward the foundation sends water to the wall. Clear wells and check that the ground falls away from the house.

Policy wording matters after a storm. Flood and sewer backup are often treated differently from a burst pipe, so check your policy for the exact words and ask your insurer which one applies before you decide what to claim.

Aurora, Its Waters And Its Neighbors

Aurora was platted as Fletcher in 1891, incorporated as the Town of Fletcher on May 5, 1903, and renamed Aurora in 1907. It was reincorporated as the City of Aurora on March 9, 1928. Homes from the early decades may have original supply lines, so a crew checks the plumbing age on site.

The city holds Aurora Reservoir and Quincy Reservoir, and Cherry Creek State Park lies on its southwestern edge. The Prairie Waters Project was completed in 2010. With reservoirs, a creek and a long-term water project in the picture, your bill is the right place to confirm who serves your address.

Buckley Space Force Base and the Anschutz Medical Campus are large employers, and the DeLaney Round Barn dates to 1902. Large employers mean many households are away at set times, and a leak that runs while nobody is home does more damage. Shut off the main water when you leave for a long stretch.
