Brighton is the Adams County seat, with a 2020 population of 40,083 and an elevation of 4,987 feet. Homes here range from old downtown houses to subdivisions built far more recently, and that mix changes how a water loss is handled.

Home Age

Older Downtown Homes And Newer Subdivisions

The town began as a stage and rail depot and farming community called Hughes. Its post office opened on April 13, 1871, the name changed to Brighton on August 4, 1879, and the town incorporated on September 1, 1887.

That long history means the older core can hold houses with older plumbing and older building materials, while the outer subdivisions are newer. A crew checks supply lines, drain lines and wall cavities in either case, and does not assume what is behind the drywall.

In an older house, a ceiling stain may trace back to a pipe that has been in service for decades. In a newer home, the cause is more often an appliance hose, a water heater or a roof leak. The first visit is about finding the source and mapping the moisture.

Utilities And Permits

Where Your Water And Sewer Service Comes From

Water damage can start inside your walls or at a public line outside your property, and the person to call is different in each case. Your monthly bill is the quickest way to learn which provider supplies your water and sewer service.

If a main break or sewer problem sits outside your property line, report it to that provider first. If water has already come inside, call the restoration line too, since drying should not wait on a utility repair.

Because Brighton is the county seat, county records and offices are close by. For repair work that may need a permit, ask the city of Brighton building department what your project requires and ask any contractor you hire for a copy of the permit when one applies.

Outer Brighton

Rural Edges, Farm Property And Well Or Septic Questions

Brighton lies in both Adams and Weld counties, and its edges include farm and rural property where the water source and waste system may differ from a typical town lot. If your home uses a private well or a septic system, check with the relevant county office about records and rules for your parcel.

The restoration crew handles the clean-up, extraction, drying and rebuilding after water gets inside a building. It does not design or repair septic systems, so a backup from a septic tank needs a septic professional in addition to the cleanup crew.

Outbuildings, barns and detached garages can be dried too. Describe the structure and what is stored there when you call so the dispatcher can plan equipment and tell you what to move.

Henderson

Henderson And The 80640 Area

Henderson is an unincorporated community north of Commerce City. Its only ZIP code is 80640, and the 2020 population count for that ZIP was 13,106. ZIP code data lists both Brighton and Commerce City as alternate city names for 80640, which is why this page covers it.

Crews cover the Henderson area (ZIP 80640) too, so you do not need a separate page or a separate number. Call the same 24/7 line and give your address.

One county landmark, the Riverdale Regional Park Complex at 9755 Henderson Road, is a handy reference point when you describe where you are. Always give your own cross streets as well, since the dispatcher works from your address.

Brighton, The Adams County Seat

Voters chose Brighton as the permanent Adams County seat in 1904, and a new courthouse followed in 1906. County records are therefore kept here, which helps if you need parcel, well or septic information for a property near the town edge.

Brighton is also home to the 396-acre Prairie Center shopping center and two Vestas plants, so commercial and industrial buildings sit alongside homes. Tell the dispatcher whether your loss is in a house, shop or warehouse, because larger spaces need more drying equipment and planning.

Brighton is served by School District 27J, a useful detail if a loss is at a building you manage and the schedule of the people inside matters.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Brighton?

Do you handle water emergencies at night or on weekends in Brighton?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open every day, including nights and weekends. A crew can be dispatched the same day when available, and the dispatcher can tell you what to do safely while you wait.

Does your crew cover Henderson as well as Brighton?

Yes. Henderson is an unincorporated community whose only ZIP is 80640, and ZIP data lists Brighton and Commerce City as alternate city names for it. Call the same line, give your address, and the crew covers the Henderson area (ZIP 80640) too.

Who provides water and sewer service at my Brighton address?

Check your bill, which names your water and sewer provider. If a leak is on the public side, report it to that provider. Call the restoration line as well if water has entered the building.

Do I need a permit for water damage repair in Brighton?

It depends on the work. Ask the city of Brighton building department what your repair needs. If a permit applies, ask your contractor for a copy. Drying and cleanup are separate from rebuilding, and a crew can explain which step you are at.

I live on a farm lot with a well and septic. Can you still help?

Call the emergency line and describe the property. The crew handles water clean-up, extraction and drying inside buildings. It does not design or repair septic systems, so a septic backup also needs a septic professional. Ask your county office about well and septic records.

Will my insurance pay for the work?

That depends on your policy and the cause of the water. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and take photos before anything is moved. The crew gives a written scope you can pass along.
