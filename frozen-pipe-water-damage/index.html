The Thaw Is The Problem

Why Water Arrives After The Ice Melts

Ice plugs a crack the way a cork plugs a bottle. While the pipe is frozen, little or nothing leaks. When the temperature rises, or when you try to thaw it, the ice melts and the pressure behind it pushes water through the split.

That is why frozen pipe damage often appears in the afternoon, after a cold night. People come home to find a soaked ceiling or a wet garage wall and assume the pipe just burst. In fact it cracked overnight and leaked once the ice let go.

Where It Starts

Exterior Walls, Garages And Unheated Spaces

Pipes freeze where warm air does not reach. Lines in exterior walls, under kitchen and bathroom sinks on outside walls, in garages, in crawl spaces and in unheated utility rooms are the usual spots. A line that runs along a cold wall behind a closed cabinet can freeze while the rest of the house is comfortable.

Hose bibs and sprinkler lines are the other repeat offenders. A hose left attached through a hard night can freeze back into the wall, and the split often sits inside the house even though the faucet is outside.

Before You Thaw

Safety Steps For A Frozen Line

The order matters. A thaw without a shut-off can turn a frozen pipe into a flooded room.

1

Close the main valve first

If you suspect the line is cracked, shut the main before any ice melts. Then open the faucet nearest the freeze to relieve pressure.

2

Use gentle heat only

A hair dryer on a low setting, a heating pad or warm towels work. Move the heat along the pipe starting near the faucet end.

3

Never use an open flame

A torch, lighter or heat gun can ignite framing or insulation behind the wall and can melt plastic lines. Keep flames away from any pipe.

4

Keep electricity away from water

Do not use an extension cord or heater near standing water, and skip appliances if the floor is wet.

5

Watch while it thaws

Stay nearby as the ice melts. If water appears at a crack, close the main valve and call the line.

6

Leave the heat on

Keep the house warm and cabinet doors open until the line runs normally.

If It Already Leaked

How The Crew Dries A Thaw Leak

The crew starts with moisture meters and a thermal camera, which show cold wet spots behind paint and trim. Standing water is extracted, wet insulation is removed and the affected cavity is opened enough to dry.

Air movers and dehumidifiers then run for days, not hours. The crew takes readings on each visit and adjusts equipment placement. ANSI/IICRC S500 serves as the industry reference standard for drying targets, and equipment is pulled when readings confirm the framing is dry. If a ceiling is sagging or a wall is soft, those sections come out first.

Insurance

Freeze Damage And Your Policy

Policies often distinguish a sudden freeze break from damage caused by neglect, such as a vacant house with the heat turned off. Wording varies. Ask your insurer how it treats frozen pipes, what it expects you to do to protect the property and whether a separate deductible applies.

Keep photographs of the frozen section, the crack and the wet areas. Save receipts for emergency work and temporary heat.

Prevention

Keeping Lines From Freezing Again

Open cabinet doors on exterior walls during cold snaps so warm air reaches the pipes. Disconnect hoses before the first hard night and drain outdoor bibs if your system allows. Seal gaps where lines pass through exterior walls, and add insulation around exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces.

Let a faucet trickle on the worst nights if a line has frozen before. If you will be away, keep the heat on rather than turning it off completely.

Should I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Gentle heat from a hair dryer or heating pad is a reasonable first step, but close the main valve first if you suspect a crack. Never use an open flame, and call a plumber if you cannot reach the frozen section.

Why did the damage show up after the pipe thawed?

Ice acts as a plug in a cracked pipe. When it melts, water flows through the split under pressure, so the leak often appears hours after the freeze.

Can a frozen pipe leak inside a wall without me seeing it?

Yes. A leak inside an exterior wall or ceiling may show only as a stain, bubbling paint or a musty smell. A moisture meter and thermal camera find it before it spreads.

How long does drying take after a freeze break?

It depends on the materials and how far the water traveled. Drying commonly takes several days, and readings decide when the equipment is removed.
