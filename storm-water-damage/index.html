A storm can damage a house in three ways at once. Hail cracks roofing and breaks windows, wind drives rain sideways into places a normal shower never reaches, and runoff pools against the foundation. Emergency storm water damage cleanup in Commerce City, CO covers the water that got inside, whatever route it took. Storm damage often looks small from the yard and turns out larger inside. Insulation soaks up water above a ceiling, and wall cavities hold it behind paint that still looks fine.

How Storm Water Gets In

Hail, Wind-Driven Rain And Runoff

Hail leaves bruised or cracked shingles and dented flashing, and the leak may not show until the next rain. Wind-driven rain gets in around window frames, under siding seams, through attic vents and past door thresholds. Neither needs a big hole.

Runoff is different. Water pools where grading slopes toward the house, fills window wells that lack a cover or drain, and finds its way through foundation cracks and floor-wall joints. In the Front Range, snowmelt does the same thing slowly across a few warm days.

This page is about storm water that has come into a building. If water is rising across a yard, street or whole lot, read flood damage restoration. If a single ceiling leaks after the storm, roof leak water damage covers that path.

First Hour

Storm Safety Steps Before The Crew Arrives

1

Wait for the storm to pass

Do not climb on a roof or go outside under lightning. Roofs are slick and damaged shingles shift.

2

Check the electricity

If water is near outlets, a panel or appliances, stay out and call 911 or the electric utility. Shut off power only from a dry spot.

3

Cover what you can reach

Tarp or board broken windows and torn roof edges from the inside or the ground. Covering openings limits more damage.

4

Photograph everything

Once it is safe, take wide and close photos of the damage, the water and the affected belongings before anything moves.

5

Call the line and your insurer

Describe where water is entering. Report the loss to your insurer or agent as soon as you can.

Our Process

How Storm Water Is Removed And Dried

The crew starts by finding where the water is. Moisture meters and a thermal camera show wet insulation, wall cavities and subfloor that the eye cannot see. Standing water is pumped and extracted, and wet carpet and pad are lifted or removed.

Drying equipment follows. Air movers push dry air across surfaces, dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the air, and readings are taken each visit until the materials return to normal levels. Industry practice for this work is described in ANSI/IICRC S500.

Storm runoff can carry soil and debris, so the crew checks the water category and treats contaminated material accordingly. Soaked insulation under a hail-damaged roof section is typically removed because it will not dry in place.

Insurance

Storm Water And Your Policy

Insurance often separates wind and hail damage from flood damage. Water that enters through a storm-damaged roof or window is usually viewed differently from water rising from the ground, and policies vary on which is covered. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

Keep your photos, receipts and written scope, and call (303) 952-8295 if the damage grows. If you find hidden damage later, report it, since your insurer may issue a supplement to the original estimate.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is emergency storm water damage cleanup available in Commerce City?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open every day, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available. The dispatcher explains the scope and the price before work starts, so call (303) 952-8295 any time.

Should I cover the roof or window myself after hail?

The Colorado Division of Insurance advises covering roofs and windows to limit further damage. Do it only when the weather has passed and you can reach the opening safely from the ground or inside.

Why does my hail deductible matter?

Wind and hail deductibles may be a percentage rather than a flat amount. If the repair estimate is below the deductible, the insurer will not pay, so read your policy and ask your insurer how it applies.

Is storm water damage the same as flood damage?

Not always. Hail and wind-driven rain that enter through the roof, windows or walls are handled differently from water that rises across the ground. Policies often treat the two differently, so check yours.

A contractor knocked on my door after the storm. What should I do?

The Colorado Division of Insurance advises caution with door-to-door storm solicitations. Ask for licensing information, get a written scope, and do not sign at the door.

Can snowmelt cause the same damage as a storm?

Yes. Melting snow can pool against a foundation or fill a window well and enter the same way rain does. Call the line if you find damp carpet or a wet wall base after a warm spell.
