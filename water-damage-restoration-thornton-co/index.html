Planned Neighborhoods

Postwar Planned Neighborhoods And What Is Inside Their Walls

Thornton was incorporated on June 12, 1956. Sam Hoffman bought a lot off Washington Street in 1953 and laid out the town, and it became the first fully planned community in Adams County. The Thornton Community Association formed in 1954.

A planned community built in the 1950s means blocks of homes from the same period. Homes of similar age often share similar plumbing, so a leak on one street can hint at what is likely behind the walls on the next.

A crew checks each house on its own instead of assuming. It looks at supply lines, drain lines, the water heater and the ceiling below any upstairs bathroom, and uses a moisture meter to find where the water traveled before choosing what to dry and what to remove.

Open Space

Parks, Trails And Drainage Near Your Home

Thornton counts 81 city parks, nearly 2,000 acres of parks and open space, and over 80 miles of trails. Many homes back onto green space or sit close to a trail, which affects how rain and snowmelt move around a property.

Look at the grading near your foundation. Soil that slopes toward the house, a downspout that empties beside a basement wall, or a window well that fills with water can all send water inside during a storm.

This page does not say whether any address is in a flood zone. For that, check mapped floodplain information for your parcel and ask the city building department. Restoration cleans and dries the damage but does not change the drainage.

Big City, Many Addresses
