Water Damage Services in Commerce City, CO

Six services cover the full path of a water loss, from the first pump-out to the final coat of paint. Each page explains one job so you can find the right starting point.

A water loss has stages, and each stage is a different kind of work. A pipe bursts, a crew removes the water, the building is dried, soiled materials are cleaned out, and the walls and floors are rebuilt. Mold can enter at any point if moisture lingers. The pages below follow that order, so you can start at the stage you are in.

If water is on the floor right now, start with emergency water removal and call the 24/7 line. If you are not sure whether your problem is a restoration job or a plumbing job, ask the dispatcher. Restoration deals with the water and the damage, and a plumber fixes the pipe that leaked.

All Six Services

Which Service Fits Your Situation

Each service answers one question. The first four are about the damage itself, the fifth is about standing water, and the sixth is about moisture that has already fed growth.

  • Water mitigation stops the damage in the first 24 to 72 hours with shut-off, extraction, moisture mapping and drying.
  • Water damage cleanup removes wet debris and soiled materials, sanitizes surfaces and deals with odor after the water is stopped.
  • Water damage repair rebuilds drywall, ceilings, flooring, trim and paint once the structure is dry.
  • Flood damage restoration handles storm water, basement flooding and sewage backups, which are treated as contaminated water.
  • Emergency water removal pumps and extracts standing water now, including after a burst pipe.
  • Mold remediation is a secondary service for inspection, containment and removal when moisture has already fed mold growth.

How Work Is Scoped

How The Scope And Price Are Handled

Every job starts with a conversation and an on-site assessment. The crew measures moisture, photographs the damage, explains what has to be done and what drives the cost, and gives you the scope and price before work starts. No exact prices are posted on this site, because a single wet ceiling and a flooded basement are very different jobs.

The records from that assessment, including photos, moisture readings and the written scope, are also what you send to your insurer. The Colorado Division of Insurance advises contacting your insurer as soon as possible after damage and getting its instructions before making repairs.

Standards

The Standards Behind The Work

Two published standards describe good practice in this industry. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. They are industry standards, not a certification claim by this site. You can ask any restoration company you consider whether its crews work to them.

What This Is Not

Limits Worth Knowing

Water damage restoration is not plumbing repair and it is not waterproofing. A plumber fixes the pipe that burst, and a waterproofing or drainage contractor deals with a foundation that lets water in. If a basement floods every time it rains, restoration can clean up the damage, but it will not stop the next flood.

On mold, this site does not make health claims. Questions about how mold affects a person belong with a physician. The job of a remediation crew is to find the moisture, control the area and remove the growth.

FAQ

Common Questions

Which service do I need first?

If there is standing water, start with emergency water removal. If the water is gone but surfaces are wet, start with water mitigation. If the building is dry, start with repair. When in doubt, call and describe it.

Do you also fix the leaking pipe?

Restoration crews deal with the water and the damage. A plumber repairs the pipe or fixture that leaked. The dispatcher can tell you what to expect.

Can I book more than one service?

Yes. Most water losses move through several stages, and the written scope shows which stages apply to your job.

Do you handle emergencies at night and on weekends?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and a crew can be sent the same day when available.
