Drywall Water Damage: Repair Or Replace In Commerce City

How to test wet drywall, where to cut, what can dry in place and when the job needs a professional.

  • Firmness test included
  • Condition table
Emergency Dispatch(303) 952-8295Call Now

By the Commerce City Water Damage Pros team. Published October 26, 2026.

Wet drywall can be saved if it is still firm, has been wet for a short time and was exposed to clean water. Drywall that is soft, crumbling, bulging or stained from sewage should be cut out and replaced. The quick test is to press a thumb into the surface. If it dents, flakes or feels like wet cardboard, it has lost its strength.

Time matters. Gypsum board starts losing strength within a day or two of soaking, and the paper face feeds mold if it stays damp. The sections below explain how to test, cut and patch.

Testing

How To Test A Wet Wall

Start by turning off power to any outlet or switch on the wet wall. Water and wiring do not mix, and an electrician can check the circuit if you are unsure. Then press along the wall at knee height, at chest height and just below the ceiling, since water travels and wicks upward.

Look for staining, a bubbled surface, loose tape at the seams and a musty smell. A pin-type or pinless moisture meter gives a number, and a professional will compare it to a reading taken from a dry wall in the same house.

Cutting

Where To Cut And Why

When drywall must come out, a common practice is a flood cut about 12 to 24 inches above the highest visible wet line. Cutting above the line gives you a clean edge on dry material and exposes the wall cavity. Cut along a stud when you can so the new patch has something to screw into.

Remove wet insulation as well, because it holds water like a sponge. Leave the cavity open so air can reach the stud faces and the back of the neighboring board.

Drying

Dry In Place Or Remove It

Drying in place works when the board is firm, the water was clean and readings drop quickly. It saves demolition and repainting. Drilling small holes at the baseboard lets air move into the cavity.

Removal is the safer choice when the board is soft, when the loss involved sewage or when the wall has been wet for days. A crew has to weigh cost against risk, and the scope and price are explained before work starts. Equipment should stay until the framing reaches a dry reading, not just until the paint feels dry.

Permits And Contractors In Commerce City

The City of Commerce City Building Safety Division issues building permits and conducts inspections, and its homeowner page says many home projects need a permit and to call before starting work. Patching

Mud, Tape And Finish

Only start once the framing and cavity read dry. Patching wet material locks the problem in.

1

Hang the patch

Cut a piece of board to fit the opening and screw it to the studs. Keep screws just below the paper surface without breaking it.

2

Tape the seams

Embed paper or mesh tape in a thin layer of joint compound over each seam. Let it dry fully before the next coat.

3

Apply coats

Add two or three thin coats, each wider than the last. Sand lightly between coats and wipe the dust away.

4

Prime and paint

Use a stain-blocking primer so the repair does not flash through the paint. Match texture last, then paint the whole wall for an even look.

When To Call

When The Job Is Bigger Than A Patch

Call a professional when more than one room is wet, when the water was sewage, when the wall is behind a bathroom or kitchen with a long-running leak or when you find dark growth. Large walls and ceilings also add risk from weight and dust.

For floods, flood damage restoration covers extraction and removal of porous materials, and water damage repair covers the patching and finish work afterward. Crews also serve nearby cities, including Denver and Thornton.

FAQ

Common Questions

Can wet drywall dry out on its own?

Sometimes, if it was only briefly damp and air can reach both sides. Hidden moisture in the cavity is harder to judge, so take readings before painting or closing it up.

How high should I cut wet drywall?

Many crews cut a bit above the highest wet line, commonly 12 to 24 inches above it, then check the cavity. Cut to a stud if possible so the patch is easy to fasten.

Is it safe to paint over a water stain?

Only after the source is fixed and the wall reads dry. Use a stain-blocking primer, since ordinary paint may let the stain show through.

Should I remove wet insulation behind drywall?

Usually yes. Insulation holds water and dries slowly, so removing it lets the cavity dry faster.

Do I need a professional or can I do it myself?

Small patches on clean water losses are often homeowner projects. Sewage, large areas or suspected growth call for a professional.
