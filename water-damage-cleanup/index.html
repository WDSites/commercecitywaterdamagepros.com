Wet carpet, soggy drywall and a sour smell are signs that cleanup should start now, not next week. Water damage cleanup in Commerce City, CO is the stage that removes what the water ruined, cleans what it soiled, and sanitizes the space before repairs begin. The 24/7 line is open every day, and a crew can be sent the same day when available.

While you wait for the crew, stop the water source if you can do so safely, keep children and pets out of wet areas, and turn off power to the room only if the panel is dry and reachable. Do not run a household vacuum over wet carpet, and set aside photos of the damage before anything is thrown away.

Cleanup is separate from drying. If standing water or hidden moisture is still a problem, water mitigation and emergency water removal come first, and cleanup follows once the water is under control.

First 48 Hours

When Cleanup Is An Emergency

Cleanup becomes urgent when the water carried anything with it. Sewage, storm runoff, a dishwasher or washing machine drain, and water that sat for a day or more all raise the stakes, because they leave contamination behind and give odors and mold a head start.

Even clean supply-line water turns into a cleanup problem if carpet and pad stay wet. Pad holds water against the floor, drywall wicks it upward, and stored boxes and furniture bottoms start to swell. The first two days are when the most material can still be saved.

What Cleanup Includes

The Work, Step By Step

Debris and wet material removal

Soaked carpet pad, ruined drywall, insulation and swollen trim are cut out and bagged so they stop holding moisture.

Cleaning and sanitizing

Hard surfaces, framing and concrete are cleaned and treated with products suited to the water category.

Contents handling

Furniture, clothing and keepsakes are sorted into clean, cleanable and discard piles, with photos for your records.

Odor control

Odor comes from damp materials and contamination. Removing the source and drying matters more than masking it.

Air quality steps

Air scrubbers and HEPA filtration are used when dust or airborne debris is a concern during demolition.

Hand-off to repair

A written list of what was removed makes the rebuild scope clear and supports an insurance claim.

Smells

Odor After A Water Loss

A musty or sour odor after a leak almost always means something is still damp. The smell can come from carpet pad, the back of drywall, insulation or stored items. Air fresheners cover the smell for a day and leave the cause in place.

The better path is finding and removing the source. Crews open suspect areas, remove soaked material, dry the structure and then clean. If odor returns after everything looks dry, or you see dark spots on a surface that was wet, see mold remediation for how testing and containment work.

FAQ

Common Questions

How soon should cleanup begin after a water leak?

Right away. Wet carpet pad and drywall start to break down quickly, and odors and mold can begin within a day or two in a closed space. Call the 24/7 line as soon as you find the water.

Can I do the cleanup myself?

Small clean-water spills on hard floors, yes. Anything involving sewage, storm water, soaked carpet pad or wet drywall is better left to a crew with protective gear and moisture meters.

What happens to my belongings?

The crew sorts them into clean, cleanable and discard groups. Photos and a written list support your insurance claim.

Will the smell go away after cleanup?

It usually does once the damp material is removed and the structure is dry. Persistent odor means something is still wet, and the crew checks again.

Do you remove the debris?

Yes. Removed drywall, carpet, pad and insulation are bagged and hauled away as part of the job.

Do you handle water damage cleanup emergencies at night or on weekends in Commerce City?

Yes. The dispatch line is open around the clock, every day, and a crew can be sent the same day when available.
