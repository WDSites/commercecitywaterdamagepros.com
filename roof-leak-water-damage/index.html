Who Does What

Roofer And Restoration Crew Are Different Jobs

A roofer finds the opening, patches or replaces damaged shingles, flashing or underlayment and installs a temporary cover if needed. A restoration crew extracts water, removes wet insulation and ceiling material, dries the framing and reduces the chance of odor and mold.

Neither job replaces the other. A repaired roof over a wet attic leaves insulation soaked and drywall rotting. A dry attic under an open roof will be wet again at the next storm. Most roof leaks need both, and the two companies can work in sequence.

How Water Travels

From Shingle To Ceiling

Roof water seldom drops straight down. It enters at a flashing, a nail hole or a lifted shingle, runs along a rafter or the underside of the sheathing and soaks insulation. Only after the insulation is full does a stain appear on the ceiling, often several feet from the opening.

That delay hides damage. A small brown ring on drywall can sit under a soaked attic, and a bulge in the ceiling can hold a bathtub of water. When you see a stain, assume the area above it is wet.

Right Now

Safety Steps While Water Is Coming In

Your priority is people first, then the room, then the roof.

1

Keep off the roof

Wet shingles, snow and ice make a roof dangerous, and storms add wind. Leave roof work to a roofer.

2

Move belongings and catch drips

Set buckets under drips, move furniture and electronics away and lay plastic over what cannot be moved.

3

Check light fixtures

If water is running through a ceiling light, turn the circuit off at the panel only if you can reach it while standing dry, and call an electrician if unsure.

4

Relieve a bulging ceiling carefully

If a ceiling is bulging, stay clear and place a bucket beneath it. A crew can release the water in a controlled way.

5

Photograph everything

Take photos of the ceiling, the roof from the ground and any debris before cleanup.

6

Arrange a temporary cover

Ask the roofer about a tarp or temporary cover if rain or snow is expected before the permanent fix.

The Interior Work

What The Crew Does Inside

The crew checks the ceiling and attic with moisture meters and a thermal camera, which show wet insulation and framing that is invisible from below. Wet drywall that is sagging or soft is cut out, and soaked insulation is bagged and removed.

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cavity and the framing, and air scrubbers may run during demolition. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the industry reference standard the crew uses for drying goals, and readings decide when equipment can come out. After that, water damage repair restores the ceiling, insulation and paint.

Cold Season Leaks

Ice Dams And Snowmelt

Not every roof leak comes from a storm. Snow melts on the warm part of the roof, runs down and refreezes at the cold edge, forming a ridge of ice. Water backs up behind the ridge and works under the shingles. Leaks from ice dams often appear during a warm spell after a heavy snow.

Poor attic insulation and air leaks from the living space make the roof warmer and increase the problem. A roofer can advise on ventilation and sealing, while the restoration crew dries what got wet.

Pricing Factors

What Changes The Cost Of Roof Leak Cleanup

The written scope reflects what is wet, not how big the hole was.

Attic insulation type

Loose-fill and batt insulation soak differently and one costs more to remove.

Ceiling size and texture

A larger sagging area and heavy texture add removal and finish work.

How long it leaked

A drip over weeks soaks framing, while a one-night leak may stay in the insulation.

Access to the attic

Tight hatches and low crawl space slow the work.

Rooms below

Water that reached walls and floors adds drying equipment.

Repair after drying

Painting and rebuilding are scoped separately from the dry-out.

Insurance

Roof Leaks, Hail And Your Claim

Insurers usually ask whether the damage was sudden, such as a storm that tore off shingles, or gradual, such as a worn roof that leaked for months. Wear and tear is commonly excluded, while storm damage is often covered. Policies differ, so check yours and ask your insurer what applies.

Photograph the ceiling, the attic and the roof before anything is moved, and keep the roofer's and the crew's written scopes. Beware of anyone who knocks on doors after a storm and asks you to sign something on the spot.

Roof Leaks In Commerce City: Hail, Snow And Permits

The Colorado Division of Insurance hail advisory tells homeowners to contact their insurer promptly after hail, photograph damage once it is safe, and cover roofs and windows to limit further harm. It also notes that wind and hail deductibles may be a percentage, so ask your insurer how yours works before comparing it with a repair estimate.

National Weather Service climate statistics for Denver list average seasonal snowfall of 56.9 inches, with the first measurable snow around October 19 and the last around April 28. Snowmelt and ice dams can test a roof for about six months, so schedule roof inspections before that first October snow.

The Commerce City Building Safety Division lists roof replacement among the home projects that need a building permit and says to call before starting. Ask your roofer about the permit and request a copy for your records.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is emergency roof leak water damage cleanup available in Commerce City?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open every day, a crew can be dispatched the same day when available, and the dispatcher explains the scope and price before work starts. You can call (303) 952-8295 at any hour.

Do you fix the roof?

No. A roofer repairs the roof. The restoration crew handles the water inside: extraction, wet insulation, ceilings and drying. Call both so the opening is closed and the interior is dried.

Can a tarp stop the leak until the roof is fixed?

A properly secured tarp or temporary cover can reduce further water entry. Ask a roofer to install it, because climbing a wet roof is dangerous.

Should I let a bulging ceiling drain?

Do not stand under it. A small controlled hole over a bucket is the safest release, and a crew can do it. A ceiling that is bulging may fall without warning.

Will my insurer cover a hail leak?

It depends on your policy. The Colorado Division of Insurance advises contacting your insurer promptly, photographing damage and covering openings. Ask about your hail deductible.

Does a new roof in Commerce City need a permit?

The Commerce City Building Safety Division lists roof replacement among the projects that need a building permit. Call 303-289-3790 or ask your roofer to confirm.
