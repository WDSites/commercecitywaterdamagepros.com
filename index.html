Water Damage Restoration In Commerce City, CO

A pipe bursts under a sink, a storm pushes water into a basement window well, or a ceiling stain keeps growing after the rain. In each case the clock starts when the water arrives. This is a 24/7 water damage restoration service for Commerce City, Colorado, with crews who extract water, dry the structure, clean and sanitize, and rebuild.

Commerce City is in Adams County, and it grew along the South Platte River valley. The city's own history page describes the John D. Henderson ranch and trading post of 1859 as the first permanent settlement in that valley, and the city incorporated in 1952. Neighborhoods such as Rose Hill and Adams City are among its older parts, so a crew in those areas should expect original plumbing and older materials, and checks them on site.

Water service in most of the city comes from the South Adams County Water and Sanitation District, not from the city itself. That matters in an emergency, because a break in the public main goes to the district, and a leak inside your walls goes to a restoration crew.

Know The Water

Three Kinds Of Water, Three Kinds Of Response

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard groups water by contamination level, and the category sets what is kept and what is removed.

Water categories and typical response
CategoryCommon sourceTypical response
Category 1Broken supply line, overflowing sinkExtract and dry, often saving drywall and carpet
Category 2Washing machine or dishwasher drain, sump overflowRemove porous materials such as pad, clean and dry the rest
Category 3Sewage, storm and river flood waterRemove porous materials, sanitize and dry the structure

Water that sits for a day or more can move to a worse category. Many of the pages below go deeper, starting with emergency water removal and flood damage restoration.

FAQ

Questions Before You Call

How fast can a crew reach Commerce City after I call?

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and a crew can be sent the same day when available. No arrival time is promised, because traffic, weather and other calls change it. The dispatcher tells you what to do safely while you wait.

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Commerce City?

Yes. Burst pipes and floods do not keep business hours, so the line answers at night, on weekends and on holidays. Call, describe the source and where the water is, and a crew plans the response.

What should I do before the crew arrives?

Stop the water source if it is safe, and close the main water valve for a supply line. Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and reachable. Lift valuables, keep children and pets out of contaminated water, and take photos for your insurer.

Will insurance cover my water damage?

Sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe, is often covered, and slow leaks and neglect often are not. Flood from rising water is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer. The crew provides photos, moisture readings and a written scope you can send to your insurer.

How is the price set?

The scope decides it. After the on-site assessment the crew explains what will be done, what drives the cost and the price before work starts. Cost depends on the water category, the area, the materials, the drying time and the rebuild scope.

How long does drying take?

Many jobs take several days. Air movers and dehumidifiers run while the crew checks moisture readings each day, and drying is done when the structure reads dry. The drying equipment is loud but should be left running unless the crew says otherwise.

Is water damage restoration the same as basement waterproofing?

No. Restoration removes water, dries and repairs damage that has already happened. Waterproofing changes grading, drainage or foundation conditions to keep water out. If a basement floods repeatedly, ask a waterproofing or drainage contractor about the cause after the cleanup.

Do you handle mold after a leak?

Yes. Mold remediation is a secondary service. A crew finds the moisture, contains the area, removes the growth and dries the structure, following the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard. Questions about health belong with a physician.
