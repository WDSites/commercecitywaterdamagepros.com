What Drives Basement Mold Removal Cost in Commerce City, CO?

No two basements price the same. Here are the factors that move a quote up or down, so you can read an estimate with confidence.

  • Cost factors explained
  • Quote checklist included
Emergency Dispatch(303) 952-8295Call Now

By the Commerce City Water Damage Pros team. Published October 26, 2026.

The price of basement mold removal goes up with the size of the affected area, the number of porous materials that must be torn out and the difficulty of working in the space. It goes down when the growth is limited to hard surfaces, access is easy and the water source is already fixed.

This post gives no dollar figures, because a number quoted without seeing your basement would be a guess. What it gives you is the list of factors every honest quote is built on, so two estimates can be compared line by line.

Area

Size Sets The Baseline

Labor hours track the area that has to be cleaned or removed. A single wall corner takes a fraction of the time that a whole perimeter does. The estimator will measure both what is visible and what the moisture meter shows behind it.

This is why a photo sent by text rarely produces a firm number. Growth behind paneling or under carpet does not show up in a picture, and the hidden part is often the larger part.

Materials

Porous Materials Add Removal, Disposal And Rebuild

Drywall, insulation, carpet and cardboard are normally removed and bagged, not cleaned. That adds demolition labor, disposal and, later, replacement. Concrete and sealed block can be cleaned in place, which is usually the lower-cost path.

Ask each contractor to list the materials they plan to remove and the ones they plan to clean. If one quote removes the lower four feet of drywall and another removes the whole wall, the difference in price will follow that choice.

Water

A Live Water Source Changes The Job

Mold work cannot hold if moisture is still arriving. If the cause is a seeping foundation, a leaking supply line or a failed sump pump, someone has to repair it, and the repair may or may not be part of the mold quote. Get that boundary in writing.

Water from a sewer backup or a flood is treated as contaminated and adds steps. A crew that handles water damage cleanup can often tackle the wet part and the mold part under one plan, which keeps the scope clear.

Hiring Locally In Commerce City: Licensing And Permits

The City of Commerce City Building Safety Division issues building permits and conducts inspections, and its homeowner page suggests confirming that a hired contractor is licensed and can provide a copy of the city permit. The city pages do not set a rule for mold work specifically, so ask the Building Safety Division at 303-289-3790 whether your project needs a permit. A contractor who answers that question clearly and shows paperwork is easier to compare with the next one.

The EPA guide to mold, moisture and your home advises that if you hire a contractor you should choose one with mold cleanup experience and check references. In practice, ask each bidder for two recent customers you can call and for a description of a similar basement job. A quote from a crew with that experience is more likely to list the real scope, and that protects you from change orders later.

Rebuild

Cleanup And Rebuild Are Often Separate Lines

Many mold quotes cover removal, cleaning and drying only. New drywall, paint, trim and flooring may be a second quote, or it may be left to your own contractor. Ask where the quote ends so you do not compare a removal-only bid to one that includes the rebuild.

If you plan to finish the basement afterward, keep the rebuild in mind from the start. Choosing moisture-tolerant materials for the lower part of a wall can reduce the odds of a repeat job.

Insurance

Does Insurance Change The Price?

Insurance does not change what the work costs, but it can change what you pay. Whether mold is covered depends on the cause of the water and the wording of your policy. Check your policy, ask your insurer before work begins and keep photos, readings and receipts.

Compare Fairly

A Checklist For Comparing Two Estimates

Put the quotes side by side and look for the same items in each. Gaps are where surprise charges hide.

  • Written scope with the area to be cleaned and the materials to be removed
  • Containment and air filtration described, not just promised
  • Who repairs the water source, and whether that is included
  • Drying and the moisture readings that show the work is done
  • Rebuild included or excluded, stated clearly
  • Contractor license information and the city permit copy if one is needed

FAQ

Common Questions

Why do two mold removal quotes differ so much?

Usually the scope differs. One bid may remove more material, include containment and air filtration, or cover the rebuild, while the other covers surface cleaning only. Compare the written scopes first.

Is a cheaper mold quote a bad sign?

Not always, but check what it leaves out. A low quote that skips containment, drying readings or the water source repair can cost more later.

Does mold removal include fixing the leak?

Often it does not. Ask who repairs the source and whether that work is in the quote. Mold cleanup will not last if moisture keeps arriving.

Can I lower the cost by moving my stored items first?

Yes, clearing the area gives the crew access and cuts labor. Do not carry moldy items through clean rooms, and ask the crew which belongings can be cleaned and which should go.

Will insurance pay for basement mold removal?

It depends on what caused the water and what your policy says. Check your policy and ask your insurer before work starts, and never expect a contractor to promise coverage.

Do I need a permit for mold cleanup in Commerce City?

The city pages do not state a rule for mold cleanup. Call the Building Safety Division at 303-289-3790 before work starts, since rebuild work such as new framing or electrical may need one.
