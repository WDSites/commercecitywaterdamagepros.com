Home Base

Commerce City Itself

Commerce City incorporated in 1952, according to the city's history page, and it grew along the South Platte River valley. The same page names Rose Hill, Adams City, Derby and Irondale among the places in its history. Those names still show up in how residents describe where they live, so tell the dispatcher your neighborhood as well as your street.

Most of the city gets water and wastewater service from the South Adams County Water and Sanitation District, whose office is at 6595 E. 70th Avenue. A break in a public main goes to the district. A leak inside your property goes to a restoration crew.

For building permits, the City of Commerce City Building Safety Division at 303-289-3790 is the place to ask. Its homeowner page says many projects need a permit, with a water heater replacement as an example, and suggests confirming that a hired contractor is licensed and can show you a copy of the city permit.

On The Phone

What To Tell The Dispatcher About Your Location

Give the dispatcher your street address, the cross streets and your ZIP code. Large cities cover several counties and many neighborhoods, so a cross street helps a crew find the right entrance quickly.

Say what kind of building it is. A single-family house, an apartment above other units and a business with a loading dock each need a different plan, and shared buildings may need the owner or property manager to approve access.

Mention where the water came from if you know. Clean supply water, rain or storm water and sewage are handled differently, and the answer changes what protective gear the crew brings and what the dispatcher asks you to avoid touching.

If you are outside the towns listed here, you can still call. The dispatcher will ask a few questions and tell you whether a crew can reach you.
