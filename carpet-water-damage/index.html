Carpet water damage restoration in Commerce City, CO comes down to one question: can this carpet and pad be dried in place, or does part of it have to go? The 24/7 line is open every day, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available.

Most Commerce City homes have wall-to-wall carpet in bedrooms, family rooms and stairs, usually over a concrete slab or a wood subfloor. A leak under a door, an overflowing washer or a failed basement seam can soak the carpet in minutes, and the pad holds that water long after the surface feels dry.

This page is about the material itself. For wet debris, odor and contents across the whole building, see water damage cleanup.

Two Layers

Carpet Is Only Half Of The Problem

Carpet has a surface fiber, a backing and, under it, a foam or fiber pad. The fiber dries quickly, because air moves through it. The pad is a sponge that sits against the floor, and water in it can wick sideways across a room and down into the subfloor.

That is why a carpet can look rescued while the floor beneath it stays damp. Pad is inexpensive to replace compared with the damage a wet pad can cause, so crews often remove it even when the carpet is saved. Backing and seams matter too, since a carpet with delaminated backing will not lie flat again.

Stairs add their own wrinkle. Water runs down treads and collects on the bottom landing, and carpet wrapped over stair noses is harder to lift and re-stretch.

First Hour

Safety Steps Before The Crew Arrives

1

Stop the source

Close the supply valve at the fixture, or the main water valve if the leak is in a wall or ceiling.

2

Mind the electricity

Stay off wet carpet near outlets, floor lamps and power strips. Cut power to the room only if the panel is dry and you can reach it standing on a dry surface.

3

Lift what you can

Move small furniture and bags off the floor. Put foil or plastic under heavy furniture legs so wood stain does not bleed into the carpet.

4

Photograph first

Take wide and close photos of the carpet, the source and any ruined belongings before anything moves.

5

Skip the household vacuum

A regular vacuum is not made for water and can shock you. Do not use a hair dryer or heater to speed things up.

Dry Or Discard

How Water Category Decides The Carpet

Water is sorted by what it carried. Clean water from a supply line is the best case. Water from a washer drain, a dishwasher or a failed sump is grayer. Sewage or flood water is contaminated, and carpet that took it is normally discarded because fiber cannot be reliably disinfected.

Time moves a loss up the scale. Clean water that sits for a couple of days in a closed room picks up bacteria and odor, so the same carpet that was a good candidate on day one may be a replacement by day three. That is the reason to call early, not after the weekend.

Pad follows a stricter rule. In most categories it is removed and replaced, while the carpet may be cleaned and put back.

Our Process

Extracting, Floating And Drying Carpet

The crew starts with a moisture meter and a thermal camera to find how far the water traveled. Then a truck-mounted or portable extraction unit pulls water from the carpet and pad, working in overlapping passes until the water coming up runs clear and light.

If the carpet is a good candidate, it is floated. Floating means releasing it from the tack strip along a wall, lifting it, and supporting it on air so the pad can be removed and the subfloor dried underneath. Air movers push air across the back of the carpet and the floor, and dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the room.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the industry reference standard for professional water damage restoration, and drying goals are written against it. Readings are taken each visit and the equipment stays until the subfloor, tack strip and baseboards read dry. Then the carpet is re-stretched and tacked back, or removed and replaced.

Underneath

Subfloor, Tack Strip And Baseboards

Over a concrete slab, water tends to spread along the surface and sit in the low spots. Over a wood subfloor, it soaks into the boards and seams and can leave them swollen. Both need to read dry before new pad goes down.

Tack strip is thin wood with sharp pins, and wet strip can stain carpet from below or rot at the wall. Baseboards are often pulled and set aside so air can reach the wall base. If drywall behind the baseboard is wet, it is cut out above the water line, and water damage repair handles the rebuild once everything is dry.

Insurance

Carpet Claims And Your Policy

Whether a policy responds to wet carpet often depends on how the water arrived. A sudden event such as a burst line or an appliance failure is treated differently from a slow leak that went on for weeks, and flood water is usually handled under separate coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer which applies to you.

Keep the removed pad or a photo of it, since it shows the extent of the loss. Save receipts for equipment, repairs and any replacement carpet.

Mistakes that cost carpet

Waiting for it to dry on its own is the big one, since the pad stays wet. Running a fan only across the top, steam cleaning a soaked carpet, and pulling up pad without checking the water category all cause trouble later. Matching replacement carpet to the rest of a room is also harder than most people expect, so ask about the whole room early.

Carpet Water Damage Notes For Commerce City

The EPA guide to mold and moisture states that mold does not grow without water or moisture, and that you have to fix the water problem as part of cleanup. For carpet, that means the pad and subfloor must be dried, because cleaning the top only leaves the damp layer in place. FAQ

Common Questions

Is emergency carpet water damage restoration available in Commerce City?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open every day, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available. The dispatcher explains the scope and price before any work starts.

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, if the water was clean and the carpet is extracted and dried early. The carpet is lifted and floated while the pad is removed. Contaminated water or a long wait usually means replacement.

Do I have to replace the carpet pad?

In most cases yes. Pad holds water and dries slowly, and it is inexpensive next to the damage it can cause. The carpet itself may be cleaned and reinstalled.

How do I know if the subfloor is wet under dry-feeling carpet?

You cannot tell by touch. A moisture meter and a thermal camera show moisture in the pad and subfloor, so ask the crew to take readings before the carpet is put back.

What about wet stair carpet?

Water runs down stairs and collects at the bottom, and carpet wrapped over the nose of each tread is harder to lift. The crew checks the treads, risers and the ceiling or wall below.

Why does the carpet smell after it dried?

A lingering odor usually means the pad or subfloor stayed damp. The EPA says moisture is what lets mold grow, so call back for a moisture check instead of masking the smell.
