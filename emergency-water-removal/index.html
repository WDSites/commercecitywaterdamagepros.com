If there is standing water in your home, garage or business in Commerce City, call now. Emergency water removal, also called water extraction, uses pumps and extraction units to pull water out of the building as fast as it can be done safely. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available.

While you wait, find and stop the source if it is a supply line or fixture, and close the main water valve if you cannot isolate it. Turn off power only if the panel is dry and you can reach it while standing on a dry surface. Lift valuables, and keep people and pets away from deep or dirty water.

Extraction is the first step. After the water is out, water mitigation dries the structure and water damage cleanup handles contaminated material.

Urgent Situations

When Standing Water Is An Emergency

Water counts as an emergency when it is spreading, when it covers more than a small area, when it is deeper than a few inches, or when it is touching electrical outlets, appliances or the panel. It is also an emergency when its source is a sewer, a sump failure or a line you cannot shut off.

Electrical and gas safety come before restoration. If water reaches the panel, outlets or appliances, stay out, call 911 or the utility, and leave the building if you smell gas. Call the restoration line after the immediate danger is handled.

Do not wade into water near electricity

Water and electricity can be deadly together. If the basement floor is wet and outlets, a panel or appliances are in the water, stay out and call your electric utility or 911. Cut power only if you can reach the panel while standing on a dry surface.

Equipment

What Does The Work

Submersible pumps handle standing water in basements and crawl spaces. Truck-mounted or portable extraction units pull water out of carpet, pad and hard floors. Wet vacuums finish edges and corners. Afterward, moisture meters and thermal cameras show where water remains behind baseboards and under flooring.

The crew also decides what must be pulled up. Carpet that is clean and was wet for a short time may be extracted and dried in place. Pad is often removed. Water that came from a drain or sewer changes those decisions, and the crew treats it as contaminated.

Burst Pipes

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A burst pipe can put hundreds of gallons into a house in a short time. Shut off the main water valve first, then open a faucet at the lowest point to drain remaining water from the lines. Turn off power to affected rooms only if it is safe, and call the 24/7 line.

The Colorado Division of Insurance says burst pipes and the resulting water damage are generally covered by homeowners and renters policies, and advises contacting your insurer as soon as possible after the damage. It also says to get the company's instructions before making repairs, so take photos, stop the water, and keep receipts.

Winter is the usual season. For Denver, NWS Boulder lists an average first freeze of October 7 and an average last freeze of May 5, which means unheated garages, exterior walls and vacant properties are at risk for much of the year. A burst line behind a wall also needs drying, so the job continues with water mitigation.

FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can standing water be removed?

Extraction begins once a crew is on site. Call the 24/7 line as early as possible, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available. Do not wait for water to drain by itself.

What if the water is near outlets or the electrical panel?

Stay out. Call 911 or your electric utility, and smell for gas. Restoration starts after the electrical risk is handled.

Do you pump basements and crawl spaces?

Yes. Pumps handle deep standing water, and extraction units handle soaked floors and carpet. Crawl spaces are checked for hidden water.

Is extraction enough, or does the building need drying?

Extraction removes most of the water but not what soaked into walls and floors. Drying with air movers and dehumidifiers follows, and readings confirm the structure is dry.

What do I do about a burst pipe?

Close the main water valve, open a low faucet to drain lines, turn off power to wet areas only if it is safe, take photos, and call the 24/7 line and your insurer.

Do you handle water removal emergencies at night or on weekends in Commerce City?

Yes. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and a crew can be dispatched the same day when available.
