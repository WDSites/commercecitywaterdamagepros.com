Before You Call

What To Tell The Dispatcher

A short, clear description helps the dispatcher plan the right crew and tell you what to do safely. You do not need to know technical terms. Plain words about what you see are enough.

  1. Where the water is: basement, kitchen, ceiling, crawl space or garage.
  2. Where it came from if you know: a pipe, an appliance, the roof, a sump pump, a drain or a storm.
  3. How deep or how far it has spread, and whether it is near outlets, the panel or appliances.
  4. Whether the water looks clean, cloudy, dark or smells like sewage.
  5. How long it has been there.
  6. Your address and the best number to reach you.
While You Wait

Before The Crew Arrives

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main water valve if you can reach it safely.

Handle power carefully

Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry. Otherwise call 911 or the utility.

Protect people and pets

Keep them out of deep or dirty water.

Lift valuables

Move small items and papers above the water line.

Take photos

Photograph damage before anything is moved.

Keep paperwork handy

Have your insurance policy number nearby for your insurer call.
